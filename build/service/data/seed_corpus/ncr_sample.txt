FLEXFORM PRECISION — NON-CONFORMANCE REPORT
NCR-2026-0311

Date Raised: 2026-06-02
Line: Line 3 (Haas VF-2SS)
Reported By: Shift Supervisor

Description of Non-Conformance:
Two unplanned stoppages occurred on Line 3 within the same shift, totaling
51 minutes of downtime. Root cause investigation traced the recurrence to
an incorrect torque value applied during spindle bearing re-seat following
the initial E-04 fault. The bearing retaining nut was torqued to 40 Nm
instead of the specified 45 Nm per Section 18.4 of the Haas VF-2SS service
manual, resulting in bearing migration under load and fault recurrence
2 hours 34 minutes after the initial repair.

Root Cause:
Procedure value not verified against documentation at time of repair.
Technician applied a recalled torque value rather than the documented
45 Nm specification.

Corrective Action:
1. Retraining scheduled for maintenance technicians on Section 18.4
   procedure, specifically the 45 Nm torque specification.
2. Recommend point-of-need retrieval system to surface exact torque
   values during active repairs rather than relying on recall.

Status: Under review — quality meeting scheduled Friday.
