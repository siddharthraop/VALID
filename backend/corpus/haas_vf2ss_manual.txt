Section 4.2 — Fault Codes and Recovery Procedures

E-04 SPINDLE FAULT
Cause: Spindle motor overload or encoder signal loss.
Recovery Procedure:
1. Press EMERGENCY STOP immediately.
2. Wait 60 seconds for spindle to fully stop.
3. Open the electrical cabinet (Key switch to MAINTENANCE).
4. Check spindle drive fuse F3 on panel DIN-7.
5. If fuse is intact, reset the drive by cycling cabinet power.
6. Close cabinet and return key switch to RUN.
7. Press RESET on the controller and re-home the machine.
8. If fault persists after reset, contact Haas service — do not run.
Citation: Haas VF-2SS Operator Manual · Section 4.2 · Rev 2023-09

E-07 AXIS OVERTRAVEL
Cause: Axis moved beyond software or hardware limit.
Recovery Procedure:
1. Press EMERGENCY STOP.
2. Select JOG mode on the controller.
3. Manually jog the affected axis back within travel limits.
4. Press RESET to clear the fault.
5. Re-home the machine before resuming production.
Citation: Haas VF-2SS Operator Manual · Section 4.5 · Rev 2023-09

Section 7.1 — Lockout Tagout (LOTO) Procedure

LOTO — FULL MACHINE ISOLATION
WARNING: This procedure must be completed before any maintenance inside the machine envelope.
1. Notify supervisor and affected personnel before isolation.
2. Press EMERGENCY STOP and confirm machine is stationary.
3. Turn main isolator switch to OFF position (red handle, left panel).
4. Apply personal padlock to isolator hasp.
5. Attach LOTO tag with name, date, and contact number.
6. Press machine power button to verify zero energy state.
7. Test all axes for movement — none should respond.
8. Proceed with maintenance only after all steps confirmed.
9. Removal: reverse steps 3–6, notify all personnel before re-energising.
Citation: Haas VF-2SS Operator Manual · Section 7.1 · Rev 2023-09
